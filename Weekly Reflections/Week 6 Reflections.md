Will told us about the "Ken Stanley arc". First was NEAT, an evolutionary algorithm that evolves the hidden layers of a neural network. Then CPPNs, the *computational* pattern producing networks (I thought it was convolutional as well). These are cool because a CPPN that you create could scale incredibly large with a pretty small encoding. 
But then, Ken found a way to combine his two creations and made HyperNEAT, in which you use the NEAT evolution techniques to evolve the network within the CPPN. It seems as if HyperNEAT may be exclusively an upgrade from the traditional CPPN.
