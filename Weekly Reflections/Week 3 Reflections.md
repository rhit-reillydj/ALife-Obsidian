### What did you learn from the other student presentations on the Week 3 topic?

This week, we learned about artificial chemistry. 
- The first other student presented on Hash Chemistry, a type of chemistry that uses evolutionary algorithms to optimize fitness against an arbitrary hashing function. While this was interesting, I did not see much value that it provided to the field.
- The second student talked about assembly theory. This was much more interesting and felt relevant and important to the field of ALife. He included helpful quotes and analogies and assembly theory felt very intuitive when explained in this way.
- The third student talked about sub-symbolic artificial chemistry. This seemed to be the most applicable to actual chemistry, and could be helpful in reducing the compute required for complex chemistry simulations.

### How does it connect to what you learned from your own work?
These methods of representing chemistry were adjacent to the swarm chemistry that I researched. With these different ways to represent chemistry, we have multiple opaque thought experiments to work with. They get us thinking about how chemistry could be as opposed to how chemistry is. They also provide insights into how we can better simulate and understand real chemistry.
