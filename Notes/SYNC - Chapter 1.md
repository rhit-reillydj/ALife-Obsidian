### Intro 
- Fireflies demonstrate synchronized blinking in Malaysia. How does this order come about?
-  Thermodynamics tells us that disorder should be increasing as entropy increases but we see great order in planes, stars, and life.
### Types of Order
1. Order in space
	When temperature drops low enough, water turns to ice
	Easier to understand
2.  Order in time
	Beating of a heart
	Harder to understand
	Called synchrony

### Synchrony
-  Can be coincidence
	-  Car blinkers sync up for a few blinks
	-  Church bells ring in synchrony for a few rings
-  Persistent synchrony
	-  Humans enjoy this
	-  Dancing and singing
-  Natural persistent synchrony
	-  Fireflies, crickets, heart pacemaker cells
