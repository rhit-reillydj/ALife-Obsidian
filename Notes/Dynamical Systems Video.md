### Dynamical System: Any system that evolves (changes) in time according to some rules
Use to model, predict, estimate, or control
Examples:
-  Apple falling from a tree
-  Car engine
-  Human brain
-  Disease spreads across a continent

### Linear System
	Two solutions can be added together to create a new solution
	Often one "accept state"
	> Pendulum

### Weakly Non-Linear
	May have multiple correct final states
	Still predictable
	> Ball on top of a hill can go right or left

### Chaotic
	Still described by some set of rules but need incredible precision on initial conditions and rules to understand
	> Double Pendulum
	
### Math
	Lots of it
	Matrices, eigenvalues and eigenvectors
