### What is a dynamical system?
	Mathematical framework that describes the evolution of a system over time
	Interdisciplinary
	-  Natural phenomena (tsunamis, meteors, populations)
	-  Number theory
	-  Computation

## Cases
#### One-Dimensional Maps
	Interval [0:1]
	Fixed points at f(x) = x
	Logistic map
		Model of population growth

#### Two-Dimensional Maps
	Some parameters preserve area, some contract
	Extra dimension gives rise to new phenomena
	Billiards

#### Differential Equations
	Lorenz System

#### Measures of Chaos
	Entropy

#### Cellular Automata & Discrete Systems
	Cellular automata can exhibit complex behavior despite simple rules

#### Complex Dynamics
	Mandelbrot set

#### Many-Body Problem
	Celestial mechanics

## Conclusion
	Topics may seem isolated but they are deeply connected
	Concepts like chaos, bifurcations, and invariant structures recur 
	Does not matter if the system is discrete or continuous, linear or nonlinear
	Provides us an understanding of real and theoretical topics through math
