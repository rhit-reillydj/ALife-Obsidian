***
### Swarm Systems
- System of individual agents that each operate on local instructions
- Often capable of complex tasks that could not be achieved on their own
	- Whole is greater than the sum of the parts
- Traditionally aim for a specific objective

***
### Open-Ended Evolution
- Evolving system that evolves without a specific objective
	- Often seeking novelty or unique outcomes
	- Has a human cultivator that adjusts hyperparameters and searches for fascinating outcomes

***
### Swarm Systems as a Medium for OEE
- Heterogenous swarms are a unique method for recreating chemistry
	- Different agents have different roles
- Interactive Evolutionary Computation
	- Human input as the fitness function
- Emergent Behaviors
	- Visually rich
	- Robust to environment or dimension changes
		- 2D -> 3D
	- Useful in studying human design behavior

***
### Original Swarm Chemistry
- Inspired by Boids
	- Simulates individual bird behavior
	- Follow 3 Simple Rules
	- Flocking, swarming, splitting/merging, and obstacle avoidance
- Recipe:
	- Defines a swarm
	- Set of particle types with corresponding behavioral parameters

***
### Morphogenetic Swarm Chemistry
- Dynamic Re-differentiation
	- Ability for an individual to change type and alter its recipe
- Information Sharing
	- Can send recipes or observations to local neighbors
- Incrementally added
	- Homogeneity
	- Heterogeneity
	- Dynamic re-differentiation
	- Local information sharing
- Observed self-assembly, growth, and self-repair
- Enhanced diversity in emergent behaviors

***
### Evolutionary Swarm Chemistry
- Competitive functions govern recipe transmission upon particle collisions
- Recipes mutate during transfer
- Majority function is the best
	- Favors particles surrounded by similar types
	- Encourages local homogeneity and global heterogeneity
- Behaviors
	- Organism-like swarm patterns
	- Predation, self-replication
- Less effective in 3D due to decreased collisions

***
### Applications
- Science
	- Cellular behavior, asymmetric swarm dynamics, artificial chemistries

- Engineering
